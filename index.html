How it works

Three steps. Then you're on the tee.

01

Build your profile

Your details, handicap, how you like to play. Takes two minutes.

02

Find your match

Golfers nearby, golfers on the same trip, or a member who will host you at their club.

03

Book & play

Connect, message, agree a tee time, get out and play. That's the whole thing.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Smart matching

Matched on handicap, availability and tempo. Your preferences, not just proximity.

Trips

Add your destination and dates, anywhere in the world, and meet golfers who will be there at the same time, or locals happy to show you round.

Host & be hosted

Be a member's guest at clubs across the country, or host visiting golfers at yours and choose who can see your listing. Trust badges and two-way ratings are earned on the course, never claimed.

Chat and calendar

Agree the tee time one to one or with your whole fourball, share your calendar and send game invites friends accept in a tap.

The ultimate golfing database

Courses, ranges and simulators across the UK, with contact details, directions, golfer reviews and your saved favourites.

Log every round

Score hole by hole, track your stats and personal bests, and keep your weekly streak going.

Courses played

Tick off every course you play and chase the lists: The Open venues, every county and all four home nations.

Share your golf

Milestones, personal bests, streaks, courses played and your year in golf, made into cards you can send to the group chat or post anywhere, sized for Stories and feeds.

Trusted community

Adults only, with clear guidelines, blocking and reporting, and active moderation.
