Support & Help

Need a hand with Grip? We're here to help.

Last updated · June 2026

Contact us

The fastest way to reach us is by email. We read every message and aim to reply within two business days.

  • General help: support@gripgolfapp.com
  • Privacy & your data: privacy@gripgolfapp.com
  • Safety, harassment, or trust concerns: safety@gripgolfapp.com

When you email, let us know the device and app version you're using so we can help faster.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

Download Grip, open the app, and follow the prompts to set up your profile — name, email, handicap, home course, and playing preferences. You must be 18 or over to use Grip.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password" and enter your email. We'll send a reset link. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or email support@gripgolfapp.com.

How does matching work?

Grip matches you with compatible playing partners based on your handicap, general area, and availability. Your precise location is never shown to other users.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the user's profile or your chat with them, tap the menu, and choose Report or Block. For serious safety concerns, email safety@gripgolfapp.com and we'll act quickly.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings in the app and choose Delete Account. This removes your profile and data within 30 days, except where law requires us to keep certain records. See our account deletion page for full details, or email privacy@gripgolfapp.com to request deletion.

The app isn't working properly

First, make sure you're on the latest version and try restarting the app. If the problem continues, email support@gripgolfapp.com with a description of what happened and a screenshot if you can.
