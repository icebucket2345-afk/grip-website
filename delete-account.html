Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Grip account and personal data at any time.

Last updated · October 2026

Option 1 · Delete in the app

  1. Open Grip and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm it's you, with your password or Apple or Google sign-in, then confirm deletion.

Your account is deleted straight away and cannot be recovered.

Option 2 · Request by email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email privacy@gripgolfapp.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify the request and confirm once your account is deleted.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account, profile and photographs
  • Messages you have sent, and your conversations
  • Friend connections and message requests
  • Trips, calendar events, game invites and tee-time activity
  • Rounds, scores, practice sessions and course reviews
  • Courses played and course challenges
  • Hosting listings, reciprocal games and the ratings you have given and received
  • Notification settings, golf reminders and device push tokens

We keep only the limited records we are required to keep by law. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Questions about your data? Contact privacy@gripgolfapp.com.
