Privacy Policy

How we handle your data at Grip. Written to be read.

Last updated · April 2026

1. What we collect

When you use Grip, we collect the information you give us when you create your profile — name, email, handicap, home course, playing preferences — and the activity data needed to match you with other golfers (location region, availability, messages).

2. How we use it

We use your data to match you with compatible playing partners, deliver the app's features (messaging, courses, trips), keep the community safe, and communicate service updates. We do not sell your data.

3. Who sees it

Other Grip users see the profile fields you choose to show — usually first name, handicap, and general area. Precise location is never shown. Service providers (hosting, analytics) see only what they need to keep the app running, under contract.

4. Your rights

  • Access a copy of your data
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your account and everything tied to it
  • Object to specific processing

Email privacy@gripgolfapp.com and we'll action within 30 days.

5. Retention

Active accounts: kept while in use. Deleted accounts: purged within 30 days, except where law requires retention (e.g. safety reports).

6. Children

Grip is 18+. We don't knowingly process data from under-18s.

7. Contact

Questions? privacy@gripgolfapp.com.
