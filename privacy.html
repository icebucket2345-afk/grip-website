Privacy Policy

How we handle your data at Grip. Written to be read.

Last updated · August 2026

1. What we collect

When you use the Grip app, we collect:

  • Profile details you give us: name, email, handicap, home course, and playing preferences.
  • Activity data needed to match you and run the app: general location region, availability, messages and conversations, and friend connections.
  • Saved calendar events and tee-time activity.
  • Notification settings and device tokens, so we can send you push notifications.

2. The Grip website

Before launch, our website lets you join a waitlist by entering your email. We use it only to tell you when Grip is available, and store it securely with our hosting provider. Ask us to remove you any time: privacy@gripgolfapp.com.

3. How we use it

We use your data to match you with compatible playing partners, deliver the app's features (messaging, courses, trips, notifications), keep the community safe, and communicate service updates. We do not sell your data.

4. Who sees it

Other Grip users see the profile fields you choose to show — usually first name, handicap, and general area. Precise location is never shown. Our service providers see only what they need to keep the app running, under contract:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage (EU/US servers).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications.
  • Expo — app builds and over-the-air updates.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting. Receives diagnostic data when something goes wrong, which may include your user ID, device model, OS version and the screen you were on.
  • Mixpanel — product analytics. Receives usage events such as screens viewed, features used and searches performed, linked to your user ID, so we can see how Grip is used and improve it (EU servers).

We do not sell your data to anyone, and none of these providers may use it for their own purposes.

5. Your rights

  • Access a copy of your data
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your account and everything tied to it
  • Object to specific processing

Email privacy@gripgolfapp.com and we'll action your request, and we'll respond within 30 days. See our account deletion page for how to delete your account.

If you think we've got it wrong, you can complain to your data protection regulator. In the UK that's the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU it's your national Data Protection Authority.

6. Retention

Active accounts are kept while you use Grip. When you delete your account, your personal data — including your profile, your photos and any images you uploaded — is removed immediately and permanently and cannot be recovered. We keep only the limited records the law requires (for example, safety reports where applicable). Messages you sent may remain visible to the people you sent them to, shown as from a deleted user. Waitlist emails are kept until launch or until you ask us to remove them.

7. Where your data is stored

Your data is held on Supabase servers in the European Union. Some providers — Firebase Cloud Messaging and Expo — may process data outside the UK and EU. Where that happens it is covered by an adequacy decision or by Standard Contractual Clauses approved for UK/EU transfers.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by row-level security so you can only reach your own records, and sign-in tokens are held in your device's secure keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever puts your rights at risk we'll notify you and the relevant regulator within 72 hours.

9. Children

Grip is 18+. We don't knowingly process data from under-18s. If you believe we hold data on someone under 18, tell us at privacy@gripgolfapp.com and we'll delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.

11. Contact

Questions? privacy@gripgolfapp.com.
